# Roadmap

## Current direction

The course moves from concepts to internals, then to APIs, then to real services and production systems.

## Volume sequence

1. Foundations of AI systems
1. How AI models work
1. AI APIs and communication
1. AI building blocks
1. Building AI services
1. AI infrastructure
1. AI product engineering
1. Production operations
1. AI product case studies
1. Build your own AI platform

## Build plan

- Start with simple explanations and small diagrams.
- Add one practical implementation per important concept where it makes sense.
- Connect each implementation back to the final platform.
- Keep the writing human and clear, not dense or academic.

## Progress rule

- If a topic appears in the curriculum, it should eventually appear in a project, a diagram, a test, or a production note.
- If a concept is still abstract, note what is missing and what will be built next.
